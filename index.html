Privacy Policy and Support · Last updated 29 September 2026

Privacy Policy

Sea Glass does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • The game has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your progress (unlocked levels and stars) is saved only on your device and is deleted if you remove the app.
  • The game works fully offline and does not send data over the internet.

If this ever changes, for example if advertising is added, this page will be updated before the new version is released.

Children

Sea Glass is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
